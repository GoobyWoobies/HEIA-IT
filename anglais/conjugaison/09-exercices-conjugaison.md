## <mark style="color:purple;">01</mark> · Which tense? Quick test

&#x20;

Name the tense and give the reason.

&#x20;

<details>

<summary>1. She has been working here since January.</summary>

&#x20;

**Present perfect continuous** — duration from the past until now (*since*). (page 4)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>2. When I arrived, they had already left.</summary>

&#x20;

**Past perfect** — they left **before** I arrived. (page 5)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>3. I'm meeting the client at 3 tomorrow.</summary>

&#x20;

**Present continuous for the future** — fixed arrangement. (page 6)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>4. If I had more money, I would buy a new PC.</summary>

&#x20;

**Second conditional** — imaginary situation now. (page 7)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>5. I was sleeping when the alarm went off.</summary>

&#x20;

**Past continuous + past simple** — long action interrupted. (page 3)

&#x20;

</details>

## <mark style="color:purple;">02</mark> · Put the verb in the right tense

&#x20;

<details>

<summary>1. Water (boil) at 100 °C.</summary>

&#x20;

**boils** — fact. (page 2)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>2. Shh! The baby (sleep).</summary>

&#x20;

**is sleeping** — now. (page 2)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>3. I (live) in Fribourg for two years.</summary>

&#x20;

**have lived / have been living** — still true now. (page 4)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>4. Shakespeare (write) Hamlet around 1600.</summary>

&#x20;

**wrote** — finished time. (page 3)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>5. (you / ever / be) to Ireland?</summary>

&#x20;

**Have you ever been** — experience. (page 4)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>6. Don't worry, I (not / tell) anybody.</summary>

&#x20;

**won't tell** — promise. (page 6)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>7. We (play) cards when the lights (go) out.</summary>

&#x20;

**were playing … went** (page 3)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>8. She was angry because I (forget) her birthday.</summary>

&#x20;

**had forgotten** — before she was angry. (page 5)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>9. Look at the sky! It (snow).</summary>

&#x20;

**is going to snow** — evidence. (page 6)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>10. If you (heat) chocolate, it (melt).</summary>

&#x20;

**heat … melts** — zero conditional. (page 7)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>11. If I (know) about the meeting, I (come).</summary>

&#x20;

**had known … would have come** — third conditional. (page 7)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>12. I (use) to hate vegetables, but now I love them.</summary>

&#x20;

**used to hate** (page 3)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>13. By next June, I (finish) my first year at HEIA.</summary>

&#x20;

**will have finished** — future perfect. (page 6)

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>14. I'll call you as soon as I (arrive).</summary>

&#x20;

**arrive** — present after *as soon as*. (page 6)

&#x20;

</details>

## <mark style="color:purple;">03</mark> · Correct the mistakes

&#x20;

<details>

<summary>1. I live in Switzerland since 2015.</summary>

&#x20;

I **have lived** in Switzerland since 2015.

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>2. Did you finished your homework?</summary>

&#x20;

Did you **finish** your homework?

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>3. I have seen her last week.</summary>

&#x20;

I **saw** her last week. — *last week* = finished time.

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>4. She don't like coffee.</summary>

&#x20;

She **doesn't** like coffee.

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>5. If I would be rich, I would travel.</summary>

&#x20;

If I **were** rich, I would travel.

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>6. He has went to the doctor.</summary>

&#x20;

He has **gone** to the doctor.

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>7. I'm knowing him for years.</summary>

&#x20;

I **have known** him for years. — stative verb + *for*.

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>8. When I will be older, I will buy a Tesla.</summary>

&#x20;

When I **am** older, I will buy a Tesla.

&#x20;

</details>

## <mark style="color:purple;">04</mark> · Translate

&#x20;

<details>

<summary>1. Je travaille sur ce projet depuis trois semaines.</summary>

&#x20;

**I have been working on this project for three weeks.**

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>2. Quand j'étais petit, je jouais au foot tous les samedis.</summary>

&#x20;

**When I was a child, I used to play / I played football every Saturday.**

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>3. Je viens de recevoir ton message.</summary>

&#x20;

**I have just received your message.**

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>4. Qu'est-ce que tu fais ce week-end ?</summary>

&#x20;

**What are you doing this weekend?**

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>5. Si j'étais toi, je réviserais les verbes irréguliers.</summary>

&#x20;

**If I were you, I would revise the irregular verbs.**

&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>6. Le fichier a été supprimé hier.</summary>

&#x20;

**The file was deleted yesterday.** — passive, past simple (*yesterday*). See [Grammaire 7](../grammaire/07-voix-passive.md).

&#x20;

</details>

## <mark style="color:purple;">05</mark> · Complete the story

&#x20;

> Last Friday, I **(1. have)** a terrible day. I **(2. work)** on my project for three hours when my laptop suddenly **(3. crash)**. I **(4. not / save)** my work, so I lost everything. I **(5. never / be)** so angry in my life! Now I **(6. save)** my files every ten minutes. Next week I **(7. buy)** an external hard drive — I've already chosen one. If I **(8. have)** a backup last Friday, I **(9. not / lose)** my work.

&#x20;

<details>

<summary>Answers</summary>

&#x20;

1. **had** — finished time (*last Friday*)
2. **had been working** — duration before a past moment
3. **crashed** — short action
4. **hadn't saved** — before the crash
5. **have never been** — life experience until now
6. **save** — habit
7. **am going to buy** — plan decided before
8. **had had** — third conditional (*had* + participle of *have*)
9. **wouldn't have lost** — third conditional

&#x20;

</details>
